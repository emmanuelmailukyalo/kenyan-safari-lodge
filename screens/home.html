An Untamed Sanctuary in the Heart of the Mara Ecosystem

Conceived as an unobtrusive vantage point upon Kenya’s untamed wilderness, Zarafa marries pioneering conservation with unhurried opulence. Raised sustainably on indigenous deadwood and volcanic basalt, our tented pavilions blend seamlessly into the acacia groves, leaving zero carbon residue upon this sacred ancestral land.

With exclusive traverse over 30,000 acres of protected wildlife conservancy adjoining the National Reserve, game drives here remain blissfully uncrowded. No converging convoys—only you, an experienced Maasai naturalist, and the raw rhythm of nature.

Seamless Expeditions

Journeying to Zarafa Sanctuary

Located in the serene Amboseli-Tsavo wildlife corridor, reaching our remote haven is effortless via scheduled bush planes and private charter aviation.

flight_takeoff

Scheduled Light Aircraft from Nairobi

Daily departures from Wilson Airport (WIL) directly into Zarafa Private Airstrip (35-45 minute scenic flight over the Great Rift Valley).

airport_shuttle

Complimentary Airstrip Meet & Greet

Our private open cruisers welcome you right on the tarmac with chilled towels and cold refreshments. A 20-minute gentle game drive delivers you directly to your pavilion.

helicopter

Private Helicopter Helipad

Direct helicopter transfer available from Jomo Kenyatta International Airport (NBO) or coastal dhow lodges in Lamu and Diani.

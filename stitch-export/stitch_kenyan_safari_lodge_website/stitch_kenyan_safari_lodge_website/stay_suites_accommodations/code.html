Frequently Requested Room Specifications

live_help
What is the power plug standard and voltage at the pavilions? expand_more

All suites offer 230V 50Hz electricity through Type G (UK standard) sockets alongside high-speed USB-A and USB-C direct charging ports embedded next to the bedside tables and within desk workbenches. Heavy-drain appliances like personal hairdryers are supplied specifically for high-efficiency solar battery compatibility.

Can tents be fully enclosed against insects and wildlife at night? expand_more

Yes. Every suite possesses high-density, impenetrable mosquito and insect mesh layers on every opening, sealed with heavy-duty brass zip closures. During evening turndown service, guides inspect all screens, lower storm flaps if requested, and set aromatic organic citronella diffusers.

What are the check-in and transfer coordination timelines? expand_more

Check-in aligns naturally with Wilson Airport (WIL) scheduled bush flights arriving between 10:30 and 12:45. Upon touchdown, your guide welcomes you directly at the runway with chilled refreshments before the short 25-minute transfer to Zarafa. Check-out coincides with morning or afternoon departure flights with no rigid curfew friction.

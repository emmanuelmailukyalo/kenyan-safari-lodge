1. Reservations & Payments

Cancellations outside 60 days forfeit deposit only. Within 45 days, full payment applies.

Comprehensive travel insurance including trip interruption and air ambulance is mandatory for all confirmed guests.

In accordance with county and Kenya Wildlife Service (KWS) statutory guidelines, daily conservation and concession royalties are itemized separately on your reservation folio and disbursed directly to the indigenous Maasai conservancy trust.

family_restroom

2. Children & Family Travel

To ensure complete safety in an unfenced wilderness habitat where apex predators roam naturally, our standard minimum age requirement is 6 years.

Children under 16 years may not join active walking safaris on foot per national park regulations, but are warmly welcomed on dedicated junior tracker drives with custom wildlife workbooks.

3. Health, Safety & Bush Conduct

No. The sanctuary is deliberately unfenced to preserve ancient elephant migration corridors and predator territory.

Trained Maasai askaris (sentries) escort all guests between private tent pavilions and the main dining boma from dusk until dawn.

A fully certified paramedic is stationed on-site with trauma stabilization equipment. Additionally, all reservation fees automatically include AMREF Flying Doctors emergency aero-medical evacuation directly to premier Nairobi hospitals.

luggage

4. Flights, Packing & Photography

Light aircraft enforcing strict weight safety impose a limit of 15 kg / 33 lbs in soft canvas duffel bags.

Rigid hard-shell roller cases cannot fit into aircraft belly pods. Excess travel luggage and international wardrobe can be securely vaulted complimentary at our Karen Nairobi facility.

Camera battery charging stations with UK and universal plugs are active 24/7 across all suites powered by our silent solar battery array.

Note: Recreational camera drones are strictly forbidden by Kenyan civil aviation and KWS law to safeguard territorial nesting birds and wildlife stress levels.

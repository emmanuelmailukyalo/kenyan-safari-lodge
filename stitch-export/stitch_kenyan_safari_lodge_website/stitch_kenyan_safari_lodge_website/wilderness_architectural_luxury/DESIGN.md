## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, tactile luxury standard for premier conservation-led safari lodges, tented wilderness retreats, and private game reserves. The visual style merges high-contrast editorial authority with organic warmth, capturing the raw drama of the African bush alongside the understated elegance of five-star bespoke hospitality.

The target audience consists of discerning global travelers, safari purists, collectors, and architectural enthusiasts seeking untamed wilderness without compromising on refined comfort, sustainable stewardship, and world-class service.

The design movement combines **Minimalist Architectural Editorial** with **Tactile Terroir Realism**:
- Massive, geometric typography anchors cinematic wildlife imagery and architectural textures.
- Color palettes are derived from equatorial soil, golden savannah grasses, acacia canopies, and volcanic rock.
- Glass surfaces, fine bone-white borders, and sand-gold CTA accents eliminate decorative digital clutter in favor of grounded, quiet opulence.

## Colors

The palette is tuned directly to natural light phases on the savannah—from intense noon glare to dusk bushfires and deep starlit riverbed nights.

- **Primary (`#B07954` — Savannah Terracotta):** Used for elevated actionable surfaces, primary narrative buttons, badges, and focal interactive elements. Echoes sun-baked clay, cured leather, and dusk horizon light.
- **Secondary (`#1B3B2B` — Mara Canopy Green):** Deep forest pigment used for structural surfaces, rich secondary buttons, pill indicators, and high-conservation visual accents.
- **Tertiary (`#D4A359` — Golden Ochre):** Applied to subtle metallic highlights, interactive hover shifts, star ratings, and artisanal micro-accents.
- **Neutral Dark (`#0F1612` — Obsidian Slate):** The primary structural background, deep and charcoal-tinted with raw botanical undertones. Replaces flat artificial blacks with rich organic depth.
- **Neutral Light Accent (`#F5EFEB` — Pristine Bone Cream):** Used for primary typography, crisp borders, hairline dividers, and high-legibility display headers over photographic backdrops.

## Typography

Typography acts as an architectural framework. **Space Grotesk** provides an imposing, modern, high-impact presence with mechanical cuts that reference both contemporary African architecture and modern safari expedition logistics. **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides humanist warmth, exceptional micro-legibility, and quiet reading comfort for long-form lodge itineraries and wildlife field notes.

Headlines larger than 40px must use tight negative letter-spacing (`-0.03em` to `-0.04em`) to establish bold editorial authority over photography. Uppercase navigation labels and sub-headers leverage `label-caps` with expanded tracking (`0.15em`) for timeless wayfinding clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an expansive 12-column asymmetric fluid grid on desktop, scaling to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile. 

Generous outer margins (`3.5rem` desktop, `1.25rem` mobile) preserve intentional, gallery-grade white/dark space around visual focal points. Section headers lock to a strict baseline, while media cards overlap panoramic backgrounds to evoke physical depth.

Mobile layouts compress padding to prevent excessive vertical scrolling while retaining large-scale touch targets (minimum 48px height for all interactive elements). Safari cards collapse gracefully into horizontal touch carousels with fixed aspect ratios (4:5 or 1:1) to highlight wildlife photography.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved not through artificial drop shadows, but through physical and optical atmospheric layering:

1. **Backdrop Vignettes & Dark Overlays:** Photographic backgrounds feature subtle gradient overlays (from 60% Obsidian Slate at base to 0% at center) allowing bone-white headlines to float legibly without separating from the environment.
2. **Frosted Expedition Glass:** Card overlays, navigation bars, and floating booking modules use semi-transparent backdrops (`rgba(15, 22, 18, 0.65)`) with a `16px` to `24px` backdrop blur and hairline bone-white borders (`rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.15)`).
3. **Tinted Ground Shadows:** Elevated buttons and active cards use ultra-diffused, ambient ground shadows (`0px 12px 32px rgba(10, 16, 12, 0.4)`) infused with botanical undertones rather than pure grey.
4. **Hairline Structural Borders:** Crisp, low-contrast 1px outlines (`rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.12)`) delineate cards, modal containers, and photo grids, evoking precision engineering and architectural metalwork.

## Shapes

The design system employs a soft, refined curvature (`roundedness: 1`, base 4px / `0.25rem` up to `0.5rem` for cards). This preserves the sharp, structured edge of architectural safari pavilions and canvas frame structures while avoiding severe, uninviting points.

Card corners and interactive buttons employ an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius, while small badges, input fields, and pill navigation chips utilize 4px or tight contextual rounding. Circular geometry is reserved strictly for action buttons with directional arrows (e.g., diagonal gallery triggers) and status indicators.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Terracotta Solid):** Background `#B07954`, text `#FFFFFF`, 8px corner radius. Padding `12px 24px`. Accompanied by directional arrow icon (`→`) on the right with a 4px gap transition on hover.
- **Secondary Action (Forest / Obsidian Frosted):** Background `rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.85)` or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.2)`, text `#F5EFEB`.
- **Directional Media Pill (Gallery Arrow):** Circular button (`40px × 40px` or `48px × 48px`), background `rgba(176, 121, 84, 0.9)` or frosted obsidian, containing an angled top-right exploration arrow (`↗`).

### Cards & Wildlife Tiles
- **Media Cards:** Aspect ratio 3:4 or 4:5 with 8px radius. Full-bleed wildlife photography topped by a base gradient scrim (`linear-gradient(to top, rgba(15, 22, 18, 0.9) 0%, transparent 60%)`).
- **Content Overlay:** Headline-sm in `#F5EFEB`, body-sm in `rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.75)` nested at the card footer. The top right corner contains the floating circular `↗` action pill.
- **Border Treatment:** Hairline border `1px solid rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.15)`.

### Form Fields & Booking Modules
- **Input Surfaces:** Dark neutral container (`rgba(15, 22, 18, 0.75)`), 6px radius, border `1px solid rgba(245, 239, 235, 0.2)`. Focus state: border shifts to Savannah Terracotta (`#B07954`) with a soft glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 0 3px rgba(176, 121, 84, 0.25)`).
- **Date & Guest Pickers:** Styled like luxury ledger cards, featuring minimal typographic dividers and warm ochre selection highlights.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status & Wildlife Tag Chips:** Compact padding (`4px 10px`), font `label-caps`, uppercase, background `rgba(27, 59, 43, 0.45)`, border `1px solid rgba(212, 163, 89, 0.3)`, text `#D4A359`.

### Vertical Explore Strip (Scroll Wayfinding)
- Fixed vertical bar along card margins featuring tracked label typography (`EXPLORE MORE`) rotated 90 degrees with an animated progress line that tracks slider progression.
